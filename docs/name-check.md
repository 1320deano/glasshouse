Method: RDAP and DNS for domains, TMview (GB, EU, US offices) for trademarks, npm registry, GitHub and X for handles.
Not verified directly: the UK IPO register (behind a bot check) and USPTO TESS. TMview mirrors both.

## Verdict

| Name | Verdict | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Glasshouse** | Fine as the working codename. Weak as the launch brand. Decide before Phase 4 (landing page). | Medium: crowded name, two products in or near this niche |
| **Lookout** | Do not use. | High: Lookout Inc. holds live word marks in classes 9 and 42 in the UK, EU and US, plus Amazon and Google |

## Why Glasshouse is crowded

- **glasshouse.sh**: UK developer-facing SaaS (corporate intelligence) with CLI, SDK, MCP; GitHub org `glasshousehq`. Same country, same audience style, already uses the "HQ" suffix.
- **Provn-Inc/glasshouse** (GitHub, August 2026): turns Claude Code, Codex and Cursor history into a "Wrapped"-style report. Tiny, but the exact niche and the exact three tools.
- **@takk/glasshouse** (npm, July 2026): agent observability.
- **Glasshouse Venture Studio** owns the `@glasshouse` npm scope.
- **Glass** (glasshq.ai): AI agent observability. Different word, confusable.

## Trademark position (better than the market position)

- The only live GLASSHOUSE word mark in class 42 (WPP, EUTM 015493141) expired 2 June 2026 with no renewal recorded. Late renewal possible until 2 December 2026. Recheck after that date.
- No live UK or US GLASSHOUSE word mark in classes 9 or 42 found. The real exposure is passing-off against glasshouse.sh in the UK, not the register.
